Governed Marketing Automation

ISIC 6201 · Open Blueprint

The problem: Marketing automation platforms can send campaigns to revoked contacts, double-email the same person, skip validation steps, or inject fabricated lead scores—each a real compliance failure (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL) or data corruption. An LLM advisor alone has no guardrails.

The solution: An independent ConsentGovernor validates every proposed send, stage advance, and score update before commit. No unsafe operations leave this system. Self-hosted, forkable, auditable.

Not: sales pipeline, customer support, or ad-buying agency. Marketing-side only (campaigns, lead scoring, lifecycle).
